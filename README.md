## ⚙️ Key Terminology & Localization Challenges
* **National mapping service** → Serviço nacional de cartografia (adaptado para o jargão técnico cartográfico).
* **Authoritative geographic framework** → Estrutura geográfica oficial / de referência (garantindo precisão institucional em SIG).
* **Shared understanding of place** → Compreensão compartilhada do território / do espaço (adequação semântica para estudos urbanos e geográficos).

### Source Text (English)
> "New independent research shows that every £1 of public funding invested in Ordnance Survey generates an estimated £9.50 in societal benefit, contributing to more than £10 billion of economic impact over ten years. Economic growth depends on organisations being able to make decisions quickly and with confidence. Whether delivering infrastructure, building homes, operating public services, or directing investment, progress accelerates when organisations have reliable information and a shared understanding of place. As Great Britain’s national mapping service, Ordnance Survey (OS) provides that authoritative geographic framework."
